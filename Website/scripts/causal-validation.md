# Causal evidence guard

Card publication checks `caused`, `prevented`, `led to` and their supported inflections locally. Cause/result, passive caused-by, because-of/due-to, and explicit kept/stopped-from-ing patterns supply relationship candidates. Prevention is distinct from causation: reduced risk alone does not establish prevention.

Each sentence is checked against its own citations. The main claim uses its assigned claim evidence; titles and hooks use the card's citations. Mere co-occurrence, reverse direction, different actors/outcomes, negated relationships, unanswered questions, and tentative evidence for definite claims cannot pass. Source text and quotations are unchanged. Existing slot recovery retains all accepted cards and retries unfinished work; successful generation gains no extra provider calls.

Each draft validation owns a lazy preparation of exact quotation text. Repeated sentence, claim, title, and hook checks reuse that quotation's eligible relationships, including empty results, but assemble support only from the passages assigned to the current check. Noncausal checks do not prepare evidence. The preparation is discarded when validation finishes or fails; it is never shared across drafts, serialized, or retained in a global cache.

This is a conservative lexical guard, not a general causal inference or semantic entailment engine. It requires explicit relationship wording, the first two distinct substantive anchors on each side, and at least 60% anchor coverage. Small inflections are normalized; synonyms are not invented. Multiple causal links in one unsplit clause are ambiguous and rejected. Sentence, semicolon and newline boundaries prevent borrowing a relationship from another clause. Unrecognized paraphrases and missing/pronominal actors can require recovery even when a human could infer support. This guard does not turn an explicit source assertion into independently verified causality, resolve quoted claims, or infer experimental rigor.

Web/native patterns, stop words, polarity and uncertainty signals come from `causal-policy.json`; the native policy is generated and checked with the existing prompt build script. Shared fixtures cover direct/passive support, prevention, correlation, reversals, unrelated relationships, cross-sentence support, polarity, uncertainty, noun phrases and ambiguous multi-link clauses. Integration tests verify sentence-specific support, main-claim/headline rejection, six-card preservation and recovery of only the affected seventh slot. The fixed prompt evaluation remains unchanged because no prompt or response schema was changed.
